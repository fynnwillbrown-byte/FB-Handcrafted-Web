About Me

Hi, my name is Fynn! I’m a 13-year-old wood and leather worker who loves bringing the old arts and traditional craftsmanship back to life. Every project lets me learn something new, improve my skills, and carry on the traditions of craftsmen from the past. My goal is to make quality, one-of-a-kind items that are built with care and meant to last. Thanks for supporting my small business and my passion for these timeless crafts!
